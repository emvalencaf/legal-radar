# Sources

Where the legal texts come from.

* [LexML](/sources/lexml.md) - The Brazilian legislative portal used for identity, discovery and URN resolution, not for full text.
* [Planalto](/sources/planalto.md) - The portal whose HTML rendition of a statute is the full-text source for the document tree.
* [STF and STJ search](/sources/stf-stj.md) - The court search surfaces used to ingest case law, preferred over LexML for this pillar.
