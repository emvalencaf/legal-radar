requisitos-cc

De acordo com o Código Civil, a desconsideração da personalidade jurídica ocorre em caso de abuso da personalidade jurídica, que é caracterizado pelo desvio de finalidade ou pela confusão patrimonial. O desvio de finalidade consiste na utilização da pessoa jurídica com o propósito de lesar credores e para a prática de atos ilícitos. A confusão patrimonial é caracterizada pela ausência de separação de fato entre os patrimônios, evidenciada pelo cumprimento repetitivo de obrigações do sócio ou administrador pela sociedade (ou vice-versa), pela transferência de ativos ou passivos sem contraprestação efetiva (exceto de valor insignificante) ou por outros atos de descumprimento da autonomia patrimonial.

How it works

Legal Radar turns a legal topic into a knowledge graph, then answers questions by walking the graph instead of searching loose text.

Build the graph (offline)
IngestPlanalto and LexML pages, STF/STJ decisions
ParseSplit each lei into dispositivos (artigo, parágrafo, inciso)
RulesDeterministic REFERENCES, REVOKES, PARTS edges
LLM enrichLink chunks to the assunto's concepts, with evidence
Knowledge graphNeo4j, seeded from a versioned file
Answer and evaluate (query time)
QuestionFree-text legal question
Resolve assuntoMatch the question to a known topic
TraverseWalk PARTS then REFERENCES from the anchor lei
Cited answerLLM answers only from the retrieved sources
EvaluateCitations, graph paths, grounding, abstention

How to read this report

Scores go from 0 to 1 (higher is better) unless noted. The terms below are used across the page.

Evaluation

system
What answered the questions: graph walks the knowledge graph; rag-naive and rag-contextual are pure-RAG baselines without a graph, and @k is how many chunks they retrieve.
citation_correctness
How well the cited sources match the ones a correct answer cites (F1 of precision and recall, 0 to 1; higher is better).
citation_correctness_precision
Of the sources the answer cited, the share that is right.
citation_correctness_recall
Of the sources a correct answer cites, the share the answer actually cited.
fabricated_citations
Citations of ids that were never retrieved: invented sources. Should be 0.
grounded_mentions
Share of answers that name no article outside their sources (1 means every article mentioned was retrieved).
abstention
On questions the graph cannot answer, the share of answers that admit the sources fall short instead of answering anyway.
contradiction_surfaced
On questions whose sources disagree, the share of answers that cite both sides and point out the disagreement.
graph_path
How closely the nodes the traversal crossed match the expected ones (F1). n/a for baselines, which have no graph.
n/a
The metric does not apply to that system or to any of its questions.
nivel
Difficulty of a question (facil, medio, dificil, armadilha); questions without one are base.
citation path
The hops walked from the anchor lei to a cited source, as TYPE(target). anchor means the source is the anchor itself.

Ingestion

dispositivo
A unit of a lei: artigo, parágrafo, inciso or alínea.
assunto
The legal topic the graph is built around, such as desconsideração da personalidade jurídica.
concept
A term of the assunto's curated vocabulary that dispositivos and decisions are linked to.
target-resolved rate
Of the references between dispositivos, the share whose target was identified.
valid-evidence rate
Of the edges, the share whose evidence is a literal slice of the source text. Should be near 100%.
drop rate
Of the items proposed, the share discarded for failing validation.
out-of-vocabulary term
A concept id the LLM proposed that is not in the vocabulary; it is dropped and listed for curation.
